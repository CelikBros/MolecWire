## Proposed direction for MolecWire

The selected direction is a **Go modular monolith**, with separate API and worker processes, and **PostgreSQL**. For a concrete stack candidate, use Kapsora's demonstrated **chi, pgx, sqlc, and golang-migrate** conventions, subject to architecture review. Use **React and Vite** for the newsroom frontend. Keep AI behind a narrow Go adapter; a Python service has no established need unless a specific Python-only capability and measured benefit are demonstrated. Do not add Redis by default. These are recommendations, not claims that Kapsora's entire architecture should be copied.

Every MVP publication requires a human decision bound to the exact immutable revision and language; an edit invalidates approval. Keep credibility, corroboration, completeness, and publication readiness separate. Check source permissions before retrieval and generation, expose only published revisions in public reads/search, and retain auditable correction and retraction history under a defined privacy and retention policy.
